The fight

Step out of red circles and out of the Foreman's charge lane. Don't stand in front of him unless you're the Juggernaut. Overcharge is interrupted in turns by the Juggernaut, Striker and Wrecker, and the cast bar names whose turn it is. Nobody covers a missed turn unless that player is down. At half health he calls drones that hunt the Medic and coats people in Bio Sludge.
